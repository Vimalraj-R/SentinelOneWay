The service cycles through 10 realistic scenarios:

| # | Scenario | Duration | Intensity | Generates Alerts? |

| 1 | Normal Traffic | 2 min | Low (0.3) | No |
| 2 | **Port Scan** | 30 sec | Medium (0.6) | **Yes** |

| 8 | **DNS Tunneling** | 40 sec | Medium (0.6) | **Yes** |
